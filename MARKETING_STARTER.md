## 14-day launch checklist

### Days 1–2

- Create and fully complete the WhatsApp Business profile.
- Create the Instagram account and connect its WhatsApp action button.
- Publish posts 1, 2 and 6 so prospects see a credible profile.

### Days 3–7

- Build a list of 50 caterers from one city or region.
- Contact 10 caterers per day.
- Book demos instead of trying to sell entirely through chat.
- Record every reply, objection, demo and outcome in a spreadsheet.

### Days 8–10

- Publish posts 3, 4 and 5.
- Conduct demos using each prospect's real menu.
- Help every interested user finish their first costing.

### Days 11–14

- Follow up with every prospect who replied but did not book.
- Ask active users for a short testimonial.
- Identify the message that produced the most demos.
- Do not run broad ads yet; repeat direct outreach until at least 3 customers pay.
